Refreshing a clean air brand with a smart design system

Aeroqual build air particle hardware that instantly detects noxious gases in workplaces and monitors air quality trends over time.

We wanted to communicate the feeling of having an Aeroqual network installed on your construction site. It feels “aware”, as if detecting and responding to invisible threats in the air around it.

Air intelligence

We explored the calm, spacious feeling that having an Aeroqual monitor network installed on a site gives construction and remediation firms - “Breathe easy, Aeroqual is on”.

We wanted the branding to feel ‘aware’, as if it is detecting and morphing in response to threats, constantly and serenely monitoring the invisible air around it.

Smart design

We flowed this work into a versatile design system for the new website which showcases their products, services, and success stories. And a powerful CMS lets the Aeroqual team easily spin up new product pages, landing pages and case studies.
